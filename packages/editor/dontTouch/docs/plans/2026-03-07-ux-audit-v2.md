**Evidence:** `onboard/ezgif-frame-001.png` through `ezgif-frame-019.png`

**Flow observed:**

5. **Frame 010:** Side panel step advances to "STEP 2 OF 3 / Edit your page". Canvas unlocked with "Page 1" label. A floating "Get started" checklist widget (0 of 9 complete!) visible at bottom center.

- The checklist item "Export your work" instructs users to "Go to Settings to export or publish your site" — this is wrong; Publish is in the top bar, not Settings
- Template selection is Step 1 but no template browsing UI is shown during the modal flow — the user clicks "Next" three times without being able to act on "Pick a template"
- "Skip" is always available alongside "Next" — P2 skipping leads directly to an empty canvas with no guidance

## Section B: Prioritized Issue List

| ID | Title | Severity | Lens | Screenshot Evidence | Observation → Impact → Root Cause |

## Section C: Recommended Panel/Tabs Architecture

### Current Architecture (As-Observed)

```
Left Rail (9 items):
  [Icon]  Templates
  [Icon]  Pages
  [Icon]  Build (Add Elements)
  [Icon]  Media
  [Icon]  Design
  [Icon]  Config (→ Settings)
  [Icon]  Layers
  [Icon]  History (→ Versions)

Right Inspector (3 main tabs):
  Layout & Size  [7 sub-tabs + accordions]
  Style          [6 sub-tabs + accordions]
  Advanced       [3 sub-tabs + accordions]
```

**Problems with current IA:**
1. Rail items do not map to user mental model jobs ("I want to add", "I want to style", "I want to manage")
2. "Config" ≠ "Settings" (naming mismatch)
3. "History" ≠ "Versions" (naming mismatch)
4. Layers and History are at the bottom but used frequently during editing
5. Inspector has 3 levels of navigation in a narrow panel
