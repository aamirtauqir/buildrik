## Collaboration architecture conclusions

- There are three distinct asynchronous collaboration contexts: internal workspace review/approval, account-less external client sign-off, and site comments. They share some persistence but have different identities and access paths.
- External review is a frozen submitted snapshot with token-scoped access. Internal review is workspace-admin/site-editor workflow. A client comment is not an internal reply, and internal comments are not exposed through the client’s reviewer-filtered list.
- The comment schema is a flat site comment record with optional canvas anchor. “Reply” is the current Review UI label/flow, not a parent-child thread contract. Structured mentions, recipient fan-out and review-round linkage are absent from the traced contract.
- Workspace Team membership/invitations and per-site grants are not owned by Review. Published-site sharing is also separate: it carries a site link to the published destination, while review exposes the submitted snapshot.
- Realtime room presence/events are a separate, conditional engine capability. It is not proof of async review, comment threading, persistence convergence or collaboration security.
- Site Review panel placement remains IA-C02 / IA-005’s product decision. No placement recommendation is promoted here.
