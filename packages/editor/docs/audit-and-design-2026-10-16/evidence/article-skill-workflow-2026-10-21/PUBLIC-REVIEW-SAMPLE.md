# Public review sample — 21 October 2026

Forty-one individually authored public reviews were read; 40 self-reported customer experiences were included (20 per product). Start with the 20 visible entries in each page's “Most recent” order; exclude Webflow's explicit noncustomer and replace it with the first eligible, previously unseen entry on page 2. This is a convenience sample, not a representative satisfaction study. Updated reviews may appear out of experience-date order. We did not use Trustpilot's AI-generated summary. Reports are unverified customer allegations/codes describe what reviewers reported, not established product defects. No quotes or full review text are reproduced.

Dates below are experience dates (ISO format)/initials and date identify the visible entries. Stars are metadata, not a Buildrick score. These are external reviews, **not the five R1 participant interviews**.

## Implications to validate

Analyst hypotheses for Buildrick: show the affected style scope before a change, preserve drafts through recovery, make plan/usage boundaries clear, and distinguish support availability from an automated acknowledgement. These are questions for later research/they do not authorize new product scope or AI features.
