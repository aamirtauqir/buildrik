## Full text
**What this is.** A 45-minute conversation about how you build and maintain client websites, run by {your_name} for Buildrick. There are no right or wrong answers, and we're not testing you.

**Recording.** With your permission we record audio and video. You can say no and we'll take written notes only. You can ask us to stop recording at any point.

**How we use it.** Notes and recordings are used only to improve Buildrick's product. They're seen by the Buildrick product team. If we quote you inside the team, we use a participant number, not your name. We won't publish your name, your clients' names or anything that identifies you without asking you first in writing.

**Your clients.** Please don't share confidential client details. If you mention any, we'll leave them out of our notes.

**Stopping.** You can skip any question, pause, or end the session at any time, with no need to explain. You still receive {incentive}.

**Your data.** Recordings are kept until [owner sets — e.g. synthesis is complete / N months] and then deleted. Email {contact_email} at any time to have your recording and notes deleted.

**Agreement.** Saying "yes" on the recording, or replying "I agree", means you've read this and agree to take part.
