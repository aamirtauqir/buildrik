# Recruit messages

Placeholders: `{name}`, `{your_name}`, `{incentive}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, `{timezone}`, `{link}`, `{screener_link}`. Owner sets incentive and dates. Don't pitch the product.

## 1. Outreach — English
> Hi {name}, I'm {your_name}. I'm researching how designers build and look after client websites — how you get feedback, launch, and handle changes afterwards. Would you do a 45-minute video chat about your last client project? No prep, nothing to sell, and I'm not asking you to test anything. As thanks: {incentive}. If you're up for it, this 2-minute form checks the fit: {screener_link}

## 2. Outreach — Roman Urdu
> Assalam o alaikum {name}, main {your_name} hoon. Main research kar raha/rahi hoon ke designers client websites kaise banate aur sambhalte hain — feedback kaise lete hain, launch kaise karte hain, aur baad mein changes kaise hoti hain. Kya aap apne pichle client project ke baare mein 45 minute ki video call kar sakte hain? Koi tayyari nahi chahiye, kuch bechna nahi hai, aur aap ko kuch test nahi karna. Shukriya ke taur par: {incentive}. Agar aap tayyar hain to yeh 2 minute ka form bhar dein: {screener_link}

## 3. Confirmation
> Thanks {name} — you're booked for {date}, {time} {timezone}, 45 minutes. Link: {link}. We'll talk about your most recent client website, so it helps to have it in mind (no need to share your screen or anything confidential). With your OK we'll record the call for notes only; the consent text is attached. {incentive} will be sent within [owner sets] days after the call. Need to move it? Just reply.

Roman Urdu:
> Shukriya {name} — aap ki call {date}, {time} {timezone} par book hai, 45 minute. Link: {link}. Hum aap ki sab se recent client website ke baare mein baat karenge. Aap ki ijazat se call record hogi, sirf notes ke liye; consent ka text saath hai. {incentive} call ke [owner sets] din ke andar bhej diya jayega. Time badalna ho to reply kar dein.

## 4. Reminder (24 h / 1 h before)
> Hi {name}, a reminder: our chat is {date} at {time} {timezone}. Link: {link}. See you then.

Roman Urdu:
> {name}, yaad dehani: hamari call {date} ko {time} {timezone} par hai. Link: {link}. Milte hain.
