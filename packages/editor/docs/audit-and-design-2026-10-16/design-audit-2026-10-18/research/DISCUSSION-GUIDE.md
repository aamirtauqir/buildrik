# Discussion guide — 45 min

Anchor everything on **one real, recent client website** ("the project"). Ask what happened, not what they'd do. Follow the story; the order below is a default, the timings a budget.

## Do not say / do
- Don't pitch, demo or describe Buildrick, its features, or our plans. If asked: "I'll happily tell you at the end."
- Don't name a step before they do: no "template", "preview", "review link", "approval", "publish", "CMS", "loop". Use their words back to them.
- No hypotheticals: not "would you use…", "would it help if…", "how likely…". Ask "last time…", "tell me about…", "show me…".
- No leading/yes-no: not "Is client feedback painful?" → "How did feedback reach you?"
- Don't agree, fix or teach. Neutral: "Tell me more." "What happened next?" "Why then?"
- Silence is fine — wait 3 seconds before the next question.

## 0. Intro + consent — 3 min
Read the consent short version (`CONSENT.md`), get the spoken yes. "This is about how you work, not about any product."

## 1. Who they are — 5 min → H1
- "Tell me about your work — what kind of clients, what kind of sites?"
- "On a typical client website, who's involved on your side?" Probe: who designs, builds, writes copy, talks to the client, launches.
- "How many client sites are you looking after right now?"

## 2. The project, start to first draft — 9 min → H2, H1
- "Tell me about the last client website you built. How did it start?"
- "Walk me through the first day or two of building it. What did you open first?" Probe: blank, theme/starter, design file, old site, code.
- "When did it first feel like it was 'real'? What were you looking at?"
- "Who else touched it at this stage?"

## 3. Showing the client and getting a yes — 10 min → H4, H3
- "When did the client first see it? How?" Probe: link, screenshots, PDF, call, staging site.
- "How did their feedback reach you? Show me a real example if you can (blur names)."
- "What happened with that feedback after it arrived?" Probe: where it lived, who tracked it, how many rounds.
- "How did you know they were happy with it? What did 'approved' look like?" Probe: who decided, more than one person, in writing?
- "Did the client need any help to give feedback? Tell me about that."

## 4. Going live — 6 min → H3
- "Tell me about the day it went live. What did you do, step by step?"
- "Where is it hosted, and who set that up? Who owns the domain and account?"
- "What went wrong, or nearly did?"

## 5. After launch — 7 min → H3
- "Since launch, what has changed on the site? Who made those changes?"
- "Tell me about the last change request. How did it reach you and what did you do?"
- "Has a client ever edited the site themselves? What happened?"
- "Do you charge for changes after launch? How does that work?"

## 6. Pains and workarounds — 3 min → all
- "Across that project, what took longer than it should have?"
- "Anything you built or set up yourself to cope — a doc, a spreadsheet, a plugin, a habit?"
- "What tools are you using for this today? What made you pick them?"

## 7. Wrap — 2 min
- "Anything about how you build client sites that I should have asked?"
- "Is there one peer who works differently from you that I could talk to?" (one referral max)
- Thank, confirm incentive, stop recording. Answer Buildrick questions only now.

## Probes (any section)
"Why then?" · "What did you do instead?" · "How often does that happen?" · "When did that last happen?" · "Can you show me?" · "What did that cost you — time, money, the client?" · "Who decided?"
