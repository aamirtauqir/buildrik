## 1. Why
Owner decision R1 (2026-10-18) makes validation a **gate**: these 5 interviews run **before** first-run Figma work starts. Every line of 00-STRATEGY is a working hypothesis; this kit produces the evidence to keep, change or drop it.

**Out of scope here:** the 5-user moderated **prototype test** of first run (runs with the first Figma pass), the core-loop task test (after Figma of panels 01–05, 11, 12) and the 3 non-designer `/review/<token>` tests. Interviews only collect **past behaviour** behind H2/H4; they cannot validate a design.

Owner: founder (00-STRATEGY §7 text, R1). Note: the §7 table row for H1 still reads "owner: to assign" — reconcile there, not here.

## 2. Hypotheses and evidence

| # | Hypothesis | Confirms | Refutes (explicit) | Interview covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Primary user = solo professional designer/freelancer building client sites, not an agency team | ≥3 of 5 build and maintain client sites mostly alone; the solo pains (unpaid feedback rounds, post-launch edits) are named unprompted | ≥3 of 5 say the decisive work (build, review, publish) involves 2+ teammates on the same site, **or** the agency participants describe the same job with no team-specific needs **and** solo participants describe needs agencies lack — i.e. the split doesn't matter; **or** solos don't build for clients (own sites only) | Fully |
| H2 | Page template → edit → Preview is the activation path | Last 2–3 projects started from a template/theme/starter and their first "real" moment was seeing it rendered | ≥3 of 5 start from a blank canvas, a Figma design or code, **or** their first meaningful moment is something else (brand set-up, content import, client call) | Prior behaviour only — the prototype test decides |
| H3 | Core loop build → preview → client review → publish → change matches how they work | ≥3 of 5 describe these steps in roughly this order, with post-launch changes as a recurring part of the job | ≥3 of 5 hand off after launch (no "change" step), **or** client review happens before build (static mocks only), **or** publish/hosting is done by someone else | Fully |
| H4 | Clients can review/approve without training | Clients today give feedback with no set-up (comments on a link, email, call) and approval is a simple "yes" | ≥3 of 5 report clients needing a walkthrough, accounts or a call to give usable feedback, **or** approval needs several signers | Prior behaviour only — the 3 non-designer tests decide |

"Mixed" = 2 of 5 either way, or the agency pair contradicts the solo trio. Record it as mixed; don't round up.
