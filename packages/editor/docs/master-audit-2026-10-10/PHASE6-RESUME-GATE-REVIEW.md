## Action-ledger correction that must not be skipped

The Inspector plan's `IN-A006` labels an “Action / help” control as an arbitrary typed-value/computed-document limitation. Help is not a typed property field. Several plan classes also conflate ordinary editor content, current-state actions and modal-covered controls under `disabled_reason`. These broad preparation categories cannot be copied unchanged into the final audit. Classify by the actual control and visible state; use `covered_inert` with its modal owner where appropriate. A standard clickable Help, close, tab or menu cannot be called a backend limitation merely because its destination is absent.

Source: `evidence/figma-phase6-inspector-action-map.json`; final permitted classes and required fields are in `evidence/phase6-action-audit.schema.json`. The workspace plan likewise records intended rather than completed actions in `evidence/phase6-workspace-prototype-plan.json`.
