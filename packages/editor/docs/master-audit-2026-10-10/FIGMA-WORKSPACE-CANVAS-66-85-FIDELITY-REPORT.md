## Capture reconciliation

The first mobile and empty-page PNGs disagreed materially with their legacy DOM layout captures. Mobile showed a full-height clipped page while the layout described a short centered card; the empty-page screenshot and layout described different canvas/Inspector states. They were not combined.

Root supplied fresh v2 PNG/layout pairs at 16:06:35 and 16:06:46 UTC. Frames 79 and 84 were regenerated **in place**, preserving IDs `8308:1701` and `8308:3572`. Both refreshes completed with zero item errors. The new mobile frame shows a 375px page at 61% zoom, with Layers active and no left panel visible. The new empty frame shows the wide Add panel and its actual truncated top navigation. Superseded captures remain in the evidence directory with `-superseded` suffixes.

The v2 empty-page PNG includes a transient “Tip 4/4” onboarding card that is absent from its paired DOM capture. That card is not reconstructed here. The saved screenshot is evidence of the transient state, and the native frame follows the stable captured layout. This limitation must accompany any claim that the frame matches that screenshot.

## Remaining limitations and boundaries

- These twenty evidence frames use absolute native geometry and clipping to preserve observed viewport positions. Their structural read contains **zero Auto Layout frames inside the screenshots**. They are editable visual evidence, not a responsive reusable component system. Reusable/Auto Layout coverage for Gate 4 must be established from the separate native foundations/components and the rest of the AS-IS page; this review does not establish it.
- Browser and Figma font metrics, wrapping, antialiasing, one-pixel baselines, some ellipsis boundaries, focus rings and shadow rendering still differ slightly. The v2 project breadcrumb truncates with an ellipsis where the browser capture clips characters. Major text, controls, panel widths and state distinctions remain visible.
- The renderer reports unsupported sized/tiled CSS backgrounds (canvas grids and checkerboard swatches). These are not asserted to be pixel-exact. The legacy captures have incomplete stacking/pseudo-element metadata; source-backed repairs above cover the substantive observed omissions.
- The measured lower-right Agentation circle is retained as a development-tool artifact, not a product affordance. The Next.js development badge is omitted consistently with the page's existing capture policy.
- Captured defects/limitations remain visible: unconfigured CMS dynamic pages, no published versions, missing image source, disabled asset replacement, locked controls, mixed multiselection values, rough form fields and saving status. A faithful frame does not verify that its action works.
- No new runtime scenarios were driven by this reviewer. The browser evidence was captured by the root agent; source references supplement that evidence. No publish, deletion, replacement, upload, save-version or CMS mutation was performed as part of this comparison.
