Lesson 01 · Figma audit · ~20 min

Char sawal, har step par

"Messy hai", "discoverable nahi" — ye feelings hain, findings nahi. Ye lesson ek flow ko steps mein todta hai aur har step par char sawal poochta hai. Jawab Pass/Fail nikalta hai, evidence ke saath — wahi jo aap agent ki QA report mein dekh chuke hain, ab aap ke haath mein.

Method — teen cheezein pehle likho

NN/g ka cognitive walkthrough. Shuru karne se pehle:

  1. Persona — agency designer, Webflow jaanta hai, Buildrick pehli baar khola.
  2. Task — ek jumla, user ki zabaan: "Site ka primary colour badlo."
  3. Action sequence — har click ek step. Jo raasta aap chahte hain ke user le, wahi likho.

Phir har step par ye char sawal, isi order mein:

#SawalHaan ka matlab
Q1User ko pata hai yahan kya karna hai?Goal ke liye ye step zaroori lagta hai
Q2Sahi control nazar aata hai?Bina dhoonde, bina hover ke
Q3Label dekh kar samajh aata hai ke yehi karna hai?Naam = user ka irada
Q4Karne ke baad progress dikhti hai?Kuch badla, aur user ne dekha
Rule Charon Haan = PASS. Ek bhi Nahi = FAIL, aur wo step issue-table ki ek row ban jaata hai. Ye NN/g ka literal rule hai, meri sakhti nahi.

Worked example — "Primary colour badlo"

Wahi flow jo aap ke file mein do baar maujood hai: purane page par aur v3 par. Dono ko score karte hain, taake aap dekhein method kya pakadta hai.

Purana page (Editor v1 Clone) — 6 steps

Kholein: Clone · Home

StepQ1Q2Q3Q4Verdict · evidence
1 Rail › Brand✓✓✓✓PASS label visible, panel khulta hai
2 "Colours & type" row✓✓✗✓FAIL Q3: 9 rows mein se "Colours & type" ✓ lekin subtitle "Colours, type, spacing · 4" — token manager lagta hai, colour picker nahi
3 "color · 18" kind✗✓✗✓FAIL Q1/Q3: user "colour badalna" chahta tha, ab "18 tokens · used 5×" — engine ki zabaan (H2)
4 "primary" row✓✓✓✓PASS naam + hex + "used 34×" — clear
5 Edit → value✓✓✓✗FAIL Q4: "Primary colour staged" — canvas nahi badla, "staged" ka matlab user ko nahi pata (H1)
6 Save to draft✗✓✓✓FAIL Q1: "draft"? Maine to colour badla tha — ab publish bhi karna hai? (H1, H2)

2 / 6 pass. Char issue rows. Yehi wo "6 steps" wali finding hai jo plan §0 mein thi — lekin ab har step ka kyun saath hai.

Trap Score karte waqt persona ban kar socho, designer ban kar nahi. Aap ko pata hai "staged" ka matlab kya hai — pehli baar wale user ko nahi. Jab shak ho, Nahi likho.

Practice — ab aap: "Menu page ka layout template se badlo"

Kholein: v3 · Pages panel. Options mein Show hints on click ON karo. Har step khud click karo, phir yahan score karo — teacher ka verdict charon jawab ke baad khulta hai.

Step 1 · Rail › Pages

Left rail mein "Pages" click.

Step 2 · Menu row ka ⋯

Menu row ke right side ⋯ click.

Step 3 · "Replace layout with template…"

Menu mein wo row click.

Step 4 · Catalogue (replace mode) › "Use for Menu"

Bistro Menu card ka "Use for Menu".

Step 5 · Confirm "Replace Menu with Bistro Menu?"

"Replace Menu" button.

Step 6 · "Backup ready" › Replace

Doosra dialog: "Replace".

Step 7 · Applied

Menu · Bistro Menu template applied.

Aap ne kya banaya Ek flow, 7 steps, 1 P1 finding evidence ke saath — "Templates · replace | Replace Menu ke baad | applied | doosra Replace dialog | backup step confirm mein merge | P1". Ye row sidha issue table mein jaati hai. Feeling se finding tak: 15 minute.
