Lens 2 · Aasaan hai? (cognitive walkthrough)

Pehle likho: persona (agency designer, Webflow jaanta hai, Buildrick pehli baar), task (ek jumla), action sequence (har click ek step). Phir har step par ye char sawal — NN/g ke alfaaz mein (source):

#Sawal (NN/g)Roman UrduFail ka typical shakal
Q1Will users try to achieve the right result?User ko pata hai yahan kya karna hai?Do confirm ek ke baad ek; "main sahi jagah hoon?"
Q2Will users notice that the correct action is available?Sahi control nazar aata hai?⋯ ke peeche, hover par hi dikhta hai, 9 sections mein dafan
Q3Will users associate the correct action with the result?Label dekh kar samajh aata hai?"Content" jab matlab CMS; "Colours & type" jab matlab tokens
Q4After the action, will users see that progress is made?Karne ke baad progress dikhti hai?"staged" chip, canvas nahi badla; toast nahi

Step = PASS sirf jab charon Haan. Ek bhi Nahi = FAIL = issue-table row.
