# Mission established: audit his own Figma prototype, himself

Founder's ask (2026-09-14): "audit karni hai apni Figma file ka — flow theek
hai? user-friendly? aasaan? chal bhi raha hai?" Every audit so far was agent-run
(QA report 2026-09-13; v3 IA plan 2026-09-14). He wants the skill, not another
report.

Prior knowledge established from this session: he already knows the file's
anatomy (rail / drawer / canvas / inspector), has seen a Module|Action|Expected|
Actual|Fix|Priority table, and accepted "coverage = tested-passing ÷ all
intended". He does NOT yet have a repeatable per-step method — his judgments so
far are gestalt ("messy", "not discoverable").

Implication: teach the cognitive walkthrough's four questions FIRST (converts
"messy" into per-step Pass/Fail with evidence), on a flow he already knows the
answer to (change primary colour: 6 steps in the old page, 2 in v3), then have
him score a second flow himself. Heuristics come second, as the vocabulary for
the Fix column. Presentation-mode mechanics (Show hints on click, R) ride along
as the "does it even work" lens.
