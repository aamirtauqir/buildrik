# Appendix A: Enum Dictionary

> Source: `lib/constants/enums.ts`, `lib/constants/plan-limits.ts`

---

## AuthProvider

| Value    | Business Meaning                              |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `EMAIL`  | User registered/logs in with email + password |
| `GOOGLE` | User authenticates via Google OAuth           |
| `GITHUB` | User authenticates via GitHub OAuth           |

---

## UserRole

| Value    | Business Meaning                                                      |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `OWNER`  | Workspace creator. Full control including billing, deletion, transfer |
| `ADMIN`  | Can manage team, sites, and settings. Cannot delete workspace         |
| `EDITOR` | Can create/edit sites. Cannot manage team or billing                  |
| `VIEWER` | Read-only access to sites assigned via permissions                    |

---

## SiteStatus

| Value       | Business Meaning                                    |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| `DRAFT`     | Site is being built, not yet visible to the public   |
| `PUBLISHED` | Site is live and accessible via slug or custom domain |
| `ARCHIVED`  | Site is soft-deleted, hidden from lists, can be unarchived |

---

## SiteCreationMethod

| Value      | Business Meaning                                     |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| `BLANK`    | Site created from a blank canvas (empty page)        |
| `TEMPLATE` | Site created by cloning a template from the gallery  |
| `AI`       | Site generated by the AI generation pipeline         |

---

## InviteStatus

| Value      | Business Meaning                                       |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| `PENDING`  | Invitation sent but not yet acted upon                 |
| `ACCEPTED` | Invitee accepted and joined the workspace              |
| `DECLINED` | Invitee explicitly declined the invitation             |
| `EXPIRED`  | Invitation passed its expiration date without action   |

---

## MemberStatus

| Value       | Business Meaning                                    |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| `ACTIVE`    | Member has full access according to their role      |
| `SUSPENDED` | Member's access has been temporarily revoked        |

---

## SubscriptionPlan

| Value      | Business Meaning                              |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `FREE`     | No-cost tier with limited quotas              |
| `PRO`      | Mid-tier paid plan for freelancers/small teams |
| `BUSINESS` | High-tier plan for agencies with generous/unlimited quotas |

---

## SubscriptionStatus

| Value        | Business Meaning                                              |
|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| `ACTIVE`     | Subscription is current and fully paid                        |
| `PAST_DUE`   | Payment failed; dunning grace period active                   |
| `CANCELLED`  | Subscription has been cancelled (may still be in grace period) |
| `INCOMPLETE` | Initial payment never completed; subscription not yet active  |

---

## BillingInterval

| Value     | Business Meaning                              |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `MONTHLY` | Subscription billed every calendar month      |
| `YEARLY`  | Subscription billed annually (discounted rate) |

---

## InvoiceStatus

| Value      | Business Meaning                            |
|------------|---------------------------------------------|
| `PAID`     | Payment collected successfully              |
| `FAILED`   | Payment attempt failed                      |
| `PENDING`  | Invoice issued but payment not yet attempted |
| `REFUNDED` | Payment was reversed/refunded               |

---

## DomainStatus

| Value      | Business Meaning                                    |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| `PENDING`  | Domain connected but DNS records not yet verified   |
| `VERIFIED` | DNS records verified; domain is active              |
| `FAILED`   | DNS verification failed after checking              |

---

## SslStatus

| Value     | Business Meaning                                    |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| `PENDING` | SSL certificate provisioning is in progress         |
| `ACTIVE`  | SSL certificate is valid and serving traffic        |
| `EXPIRED` | SSL certificate has expired and needs renewal       |

---

| `SECURITY_LOGIN_NEW_DEVICE`  | A login occurred from a previously unseen device          |

## Plan Limits by Tier

> Source: `lib/constants/plan-limits.ts`

| Limit Key               | FREE              | PRO               | BUSINESS              |
|--------------------------|-------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| `sites`                  | 3                 | 15                 | 50                    |
| `pagesPerSite`           | 10                | 30                 | 50                    |
| `customDomains`          | 0                 | 3                  | 20                    |
| `teamMembers`            | 1                 | 5                  | 25                    |
| `storageMB`              | 500 MB            | 5,120 MB (5 GB)    | 51,200 MB (50 GB)     |
| `bandwidthMB`            | 1,024 MB (1 GB)   | 10,240 MB (10 GB)  | 102,400 MB (100 GB)   |
| `aiGenerations`          | 3                 | 20                 | Unlimited (-1)        |
| `fileUploadMaxMB`        | 10 MB             | 50 MB              | 200 MB                |
| `formSubmissions`        | 100               | 2,500              | Unlimited (-1)        |
| `urlRedirects`           | 100               | 500                | Unlimited (-1)        |
| `integrations`           | 0                 | 2                  | Unlimited (-1)        |
| `analyticsRetentionDays` | 7 days            | 30 days            | 90 days               |
| `shareLinkExpiryMaxDays` | 7 days            | 30 days            | 90 days               |
| `shareLinkPasswords`     | false             | true               | true                  |
| `priceMonthly`           | $0                | $29                | $79                   |
| `priceYearly`            | $0                | $23/mo ($276/yr)   | $63/mo ($756/yr)      |

**Notes:**
- `-1` denotes unlimited.
- `shareLinkPasswords: false` means the Free plan cannot set passwords on share links.
- Yearly prices are per-month equivalents billed annually.
