# Is the 2026-09-07 "Deep UI and Modal Audit" true of Editor v1?

48 claims — M01–M20 (modals) and F01–F28 (editor-wide) — naming 76 nodes on
Figma page `1:3`. Nothing here is settled by the audit's own evidence.

**Why not.** The audit says of itself, twice, that Figma cut it off:
*"Figma then returned its tool-call limit, preventing the remaining per-state
visual checks"* and *"Figma returned its call limit again. Every modal has not
received a fresh visual inspection."* Classified by its own `kind` field, **7 of
48 findings were re-read this pass; 41 were not.** And the page moved under it:
this repo's V2→V1 arc wrote 804 rows to `1:3` between 04:38 and 18:31, and the
audit published at 17:58.

That combination has a proven consequence, not a theoretical one. **F05 is
already fixed.** Our own detector logged six 14px overprints on the Notifications
board before the repair and `0` after, re-measured at 16:29 — 89 minutes before
the audit was published claiming the defect. A screenshot is not a measurement of
now.

**So every verdict below rests on one of four independent sources:**

1. a **fresh read of the live file** taken after 18:29 (`scripts/figma/audit-verify-dump.mjs` → `b*.tsv`, arithmetic in `analyze.py`);
2. this repo's own post-arc defect sweep, `V2-TO-V1/.render-defects-after.json`, **18:19 — after the audit published**;
3. the **code**, where the claim is about what the product can do (`CODE-TRUTH.md`);
4. the **builder source**, where a board was generated by a script in this repo.

## What the tally means

The audit is **substantially right about the file and substantially wrong about
itself**. Most of what it reports is really there, and the parts that are not
fail in one specific way: it measured a page that was still being written, using
evidence it had mostly not re-read.

**Three separate things are true at once, and they need separating before anyone
acts on this:**

### 1. The defects are real, and several are worse than reported

The strongest class is invisible-by-clipping: a node exists, the write succeeded,
the read-back matched, and nothing renders. **F06** is the clearest — the AI
recovery route ("Continue by hand in the inspector") sits 32–120px outside its
clipping parent on three boards. **F07** cuts the History retention sentence on
five boards because a 48px text sits in a 40px frame. **F03** puts two 248×117px
engineering paragraphs inside the Pages product panel, and clones them onto two
more boards. None of these is a matter of taste; each is arithmetic.

### 2. A third of the confirmed defects were created today, with a green result

F03's notes were cloned **into** `140:2` by this arc's own plan, and the checklist
records it `IMPLEMENTED VERIFIED — read-back`. F07's repair set the width and
never looked at the height. F22's section counts were correct yesterday — `git
show HEAD:BOARD-BASELINE.json` has **0 of 29** sections drifting; today's has 13,
because 104 boards were added and no title was regenerated. F06's clipped recovery
text is entirely `2846:*` nodes, written today.

The pattern is already named in this repo's own coverage report: *"a green result
describes the rows a tool matched, never the rows it meant to match."* The audit
is the first outside reading that saw past it.

### 3. Two of the audit's corrections would undo deliberate decisions

**F28** asks for 31 Settings subtitles to be made readable. Thirty of them were
set `visible=false` today on purpose, because `DrillInHeader.tsx:12-47` has no
subtitle slot. The audit's structural probe does not filter visibility; this
repo's own detector does, deliberately. **F12** asks for a template picker that
was deleted twice on a code citation, and for a published-record count that is
already on the board.

The founder's precedence rule settles both: behaviour is decided by the code, the
visual by the board. Where a board draws something the engine cannot do (**F18**'s
"FROM BRAND · NOT IMPLEMENTED … linked", **M12**'s missing Apply), delete or mark
it. Where the board contradicts code that already ships correctly (**M17**'s
missing label, **M13**'s wrong recovery verb), fix the board.

## The one process change worth making

Every mechanism in `PLAN.md` §C2 and §C3 would have been caught by a check this
repo already owns, run at the end instead of the middle:

- **containment** — is every text inside its nearest clipping ancestor?
  (`render-defects.mjs` does this; it was not re-run after the caption rewrites
  that created the failures)
- **generated counts** — no hand-typed number in a section title or a button
  label (`order-sections.mjs:74` already derives it)
- **prototype destinations resolve to a board that is not named RETIRED** —
  `dangling = 0` passed all day while three controls led into retired designs
  (**F21**)
- **a clone's text differs from its source's** — five findings (M01–M04, M06) are
  one un-ingested plan file, `media-06-post-clone-unresolved.json`, authored in
  full but written as a `groups` dict, so the normalizer read **zero rows** from
  it while the checklist reported the boards verified

None of these needs Figma quota to design; all four are cheap, and together they
close most of what an outside reviewer found before we did.
