Why now, not after the build

Teen codex passes ne design ki coherence polish ki. Par ek sawal abhi tak un-answered: kya agencies ko yeh dard sach mein hai? Tera apna autoplan review (LR-0017) ne quarter bachaya — wedge "build the wrong thing well" tha: editor mein zero theme.* wiring, aur kisi agency ne confirm nahi kiya. Validation banane se pehle aati, baad mein nahi.

The one trap: you'll pitch, and they'll be nice

Jis second tu "Buildrik aisa karta hai" bolta — wo polite ho jaate. Aur polite data jhoota hota. Validation ka asli skill = apni idea chhupa ke un ki life poochna. Yeh The Mom Test ka core: aise sawal poocho ke tumhari maa bhi tumhe sach bole, jhoot na bol sake (kyunki tum idea ke baare mein poochte hi nahi).

✗ Pitch question (jhoot nikaalta)

"Kya tum aisa tool use karoge jo saare client sites ek jagah se on-brand rakhe?" → sab "haan!" bolenge. Zero signal.

✓ Mom-Test question (sach nikaalta)

"Last baar jab ek client ka logo saari sites pe badalna pada — wo kaise hua, kitna time laga?" → ek fact, guess nahi.

The three rules

1. Their life, not your idea. Un ke aaj ke kaam ke baare mein poocho. 2. Past specifics, not future opinions. "Would you?" worthless; "last time dikhao" gold. 3. Talk less, listen more. Tera kaam seekhna, manaana nahi. Aur yaad rakh: tareef data nahi — "cool idea / sounds useful" = polite no.

Read the signal: pull vs polite

PULL (go): bina poochhe current dard · pehle se paisa/time kharch · "yeh kab milega?" · doosri agencies se intro. NO-PULL (kill): tareef, hypothetical josh, "maybe try karoonga," koi budget nahi. Gate: 5 mein se ≥3 asli kharch-kiya-hua dard dikhayein → build. <3 → wedge galat hai, doosra differentiator (review-loop) pakdo ya ruk jao. Dono natije jeet — pehle seekha, baad mein nahi.
