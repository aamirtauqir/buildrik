Lesson 15 · ~5 minutes

You're not stuck. You're in the gap.

Aap ne kaha: "main designer nahi hun, theek se design nahi kar pa raha." Do cheezein sach hain, aur dono aapke haq mein hain. Pehli: aap already design kar rahe ho — saboot neeche. Doosri: jo "atak gaya" feel hai woh talent ki kami nahi — woh ek missing move hai. Ye lesson dono kholti hai.

Pehle — aap design kar rahe ho (saboot)

Sirf is session mein, aapne ye pakda — non-designer ye feel nahi karta:

  • Topbar mein breadcrumb galat jagah thi — aapki aankh ne pakda.
  • "Site" group topbar se duplicate nikla (#14) — ye team ke apne E3 plan ka real bug tha. Aapne pakda.
  • "Add cluster ban raha hai" — wahi instinct jisne palette vs grab-bag distinction kholi.
  • "Bar-bar use wali cheezein left bar mein" — ye aapne khud bola, aur ye constitution #4 hai.

Ye taste fire ho rahi hai. "Acha nahi lag raha" keh paana hi design hai (aap pehle bhi mera error pakad chuke ho).

The gap — Ira Glass

Shuru mein aapki taste aapke kaam se aage hoti hai. Aap pehchaan lete ho ki kuch "off" hai, par output abhi us level ka nahi. Woh faasla bura feel deta hai — par wahi engine hai. (Ira Glass — "The Gap")

Aapki tasteJanta hai rail abhi galat hai (clusters, grab-bag). Tez.

Faasla = normal. Ise band zyada sochne se nahi, reps + feedback se hota hai. Jo log "achanak achhe" lagte hain, unhone bас zyada baar try kiya.

Rail itni endless kyun lagi

Aap perfect rail ko sochkar deduce karne ki koshish kar rahe the. Koi nahi kar sakta. Rail ka jawab aapke sar mein nahi — woh isme hai ki asal user editor ko kaise use karta hai. Insert sub-tab ho ya alag slot, Media andar ho ya bahar — taste se nahi, use se tay hota hai. Isi liye circle ban raha tha: hum abstraction par baहस kar rahe the.

Designers think-until-perfect nahi karte. Woh good-enough par converge → test → fix karte hain. "You don't have to get it right, you have to make it testable." (Steve Krug)

The unstick — aapki rail ke liye, abhi

  1. Decide good-enough. Hamare paas already sabse kam-issue wala hai: C + dissolve Settings (Insert · Media · Pages · Design). Use lock karo — perfect nahi, testable.
  2. Clickable mockup banao (jaise topbar-final.html). Main bana dunga — asal rail dikhe.
  3. Asal job walk karo: "ek page banao + brand par lao + publish." Khud, phir 3-5 log. Chup-chaap dekho woh kahan haath maarte hain.
  4. Jo trip kare wahi fix karo. Insert dhoondh nahi paaye? Media galat jagah? Ab data hai, taste-jhagda nahi.

Decision > perfection. Pehla version galat ho toh bhi — testable galat > perfect-in-head. Test use ko theek kar dega.

Carry this forward

Aapko "designer ban-na" nahi hai pehle — aapko converge → test → fix ka loop chalana hai, aur reps karne hain. Taste pehle se hai (saboot upar). Skill reps se aayegi. Aur har tie jo aaj fasaa raha hai (4 vs 5 tools, Media kahan) — woh ek 30-minute real-use walk mein khud khul jaayega. Ye seedha aapke mission ke "now" stage se judta hai: craft aapke paas hai; ab use user par aim karo.

Sources: Ira Glass — The Gap (3 min, dekho — ye seedha aapke liye hai) + Steve Krug — Rocket Surgery (decide → test, perfect nahi). Engine: the constitution. Kuch bhee atke — poocho, main aapka teacher hun.
