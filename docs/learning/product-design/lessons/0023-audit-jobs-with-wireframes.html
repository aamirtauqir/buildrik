Lesson 23 · ~5 minutes

Audit your jobs with your wireframes — coherence, not correctness.

Aap ne poocha: "jobs ko apni wireframing se audit nahi kar sakte?" Haan — par ek had tak. Wireframe-audit batata hai kya tera job-model andar se theek hai (coherence). Woh nahi batata kya yeh sahi jobs hain (correctness) — woh sirf users dete hain. Aur abhi maine chala ke ek bada hole pakda.

Do alag sawaal — mat milao

✓ Coherence (wireframe de sakta)

Kya model andar se judta hai? Orphan surface · empty job · do-job-ek-mein seam · dead-end journey. Abhi, in haath ke artifacts pe runnable.

✗ Correctness (sirf users)

Kya yeh users ke asli jobs hain — unke lafzon mein, unki seemao se? Wireframe se nahi: woh JOBS se bane, unhi ke against check = circular (model ko apne against). Clean audit ≠ sahi jobs.

The wireframe job-audit — 5 tests

Har test ek structural smell pakdta jo aksar kisi missing ya galat job ki taraf ishaara karta:

TestSawaalSmell =
1 · OrphanKoi surface jiska koi job/journey nahi?missing job, ya surface jo hona hi nahi chahiye
2 · EmptyKoi job jiska koi asli journey nahi?woh job nahi — ek step/label hai
3 · SeamEk job ke andar do alag outcomes?do jobs ek mein chhupe — split candidate
4 · MergeDo jobs ek hi journey/surfaces share karte?shayad ek hi job hai
5 · Dead-endKoi journey jo user ke goal tak nahi pohonchti?woh job product deliver hi nahi karta
Jo abhi nikla (test 1 + 3, live on your files):
  • Orphan = §36 Shared DS push — tera #1 differentiator (agency wedge). Surface spec maujood, par jab yeh audit chala ds-push 5 journeys mein 0 baar tha. Yaani J4 ("on-brand") apna sabse bada step kho chuka, ya ek chhupa job: "saare client sites ek jagah se on-brand rakhna." ✓ Since fixed: ab ek ★ agency-wedge journey ban gayi (wireflows) — yehi audit ka payoff.
  • Seam = J6 "Ship & run it" — journey sirf Ship (SEO→domain→publish→live) dikhati; "run it" (analytics/grow) journey mein nahi. Wahi L22 wala "grow" 7th-job candidate, ab evidence ke saath.
The ceiling — kyun yeh sabit nahi karta: audit ne smell pakda (orphan wedge), correctness nahi. Woh tujhe bata sakta "yahan job-model toota hai," par yeh nahi ke "push-to-clients" agencies ka asli job hai ya nahi — woh sirf 5 real users batayenge. Coherence-clean hona = internally theek; users add karte hain woh job jo kisi ne draw hi nahi kiya (LR-0018: completeness ≠ correctness).
