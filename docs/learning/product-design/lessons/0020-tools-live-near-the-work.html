Lesson 20 · ~5 minutes

Tools live near the work.

Aap ne ek paini baat kahi: "jo cheezein canvas ka hissa hain — undo/redo, history, breakpoints — unhe canvas ke saath rakho. Topbar saaf rakho; us mein sirf woh daalo jo frequently use nahi hoti." Yeh ek asli principle hai — aur yeh aap ke L5 (bouncer) ko tez karta hai: ab faisla sirf "topbar mein jagah milе ya nahi" nahi, balke "yeh control kahan rahega" — uski frequency se.

Frequency sirf 'kitna loud' nahi — 'kahan' bhi decide karti hai

Aap ka #4 kehta hai: zyada use = zyada numayan. Yeh lesson ek doosra axis jodता hai: zyada use = kaam ke kareeb. Door chrome (topbar) tak baar-baar haath le jaana ek tax hai — har baar cursor + nazar ko safar karna padta hai. Iska naam Fitts's Law: target jitna door, utna time + effort. (NN/g)

Near the canvas (frequent · canvas-op)

  • Undo / redo — har minute
  • Breakpoint / device switch — responsive banate waqt baar-baar
  • Edit history (timeline)
  • Zoom

Topbar (rare · global)

  • Publish — kabhi-kabhi (par hero, #5)
  • Share / Review
  • Exit
  • ⋯ Help · Account · Invite
Note: Publish infrequent hai (aap der tak build karte ho, publish kabhi-kabhi) — isliye woh topbar mein theek baithta hai. "Topbar = rare/global" aur "Publish = hero" mein koi takraao nahi: woh rare-but-important global ship-action hai.
