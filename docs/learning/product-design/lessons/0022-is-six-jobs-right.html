Lesson 22 · ~4 minutes

Is six jobs right? — what counts as a job.

Aap ne poocha: "sirf 6 jobs hain ya aur bhi?" Aaj haan, 6 — aur woh achhe-form ke hain. Par 6 koi qanoon nahi: yeh aap ke code se nikaala grouping hai, real users se sabit nahi. Yeh lesson do cheezein deta hai — (1) job kya hota hai (taa-ke aap khud gin sako), (2) 6 sahi hai ya nahi ka asli test.

Pehle: har cheez job nahi hoti

List 6 pe isiliye ruki kyunki teen cheezein job hain hi nahi. Yeh farq pakdo:

Job

Ek nateeja jo user chahta — UI ka zikr kiye baghair. "Sign-off lena," "page banana," "ship karna." Shuru + ant hota. Dost ko aise batata.

Tool (cross-cutting)

Har job ke andar chalta, apna ghar nahi. AI isi liye 7th job nahi — woh build/brand/ship ke andar madad karta. (home-map)

Substrate

System ko chahiye, par user ka "kaam" nahi. Auth · save · states · confirm. Inhe nav/job-space mat do (#13 states = pattern).

The test — "I came here to ___": blank mein jo aaye, woh job hai. "Main aaya page banane / client se sign-off lene / publish karne" → job. "Main aaya login karne" → nahi (substrate). "Main aaya AI use karne" → nahi (tool — AI kisi job ki khidmat mein hai). Job = outcome, na ke step, tool, ya plumbing.

Toh 6 sahi hai?

6 aaj theek hain — har ek asli outcome, har ek ka ghar, top-level 5–6 (IA test pass). Lekin yeh 6 code se reverse-engineer hue (backend-map se), users dekh ke nahi — isi liye woh modules pe itne saaf baithte hain. Ek real user shayad inhe alag kaate. Kuch candidate 7th jobs abhi J3/J6 ke andar chhupe hain:

Abhi inhe alag karna = khaali job banana (LR-0005 ghost-risk). Isi liye 6 par rehna sahi hai — par jaan lo yeh ek educated guess hai, sabit nahi.

6 ko sabit kaise karein: features gin ke nahi — 5 real users dekh ke (mission ka pro-frontier · Krug ka DIY 5-user test). Woh kaam karte waqt jin lafzon mein apna goal batate hain — wohi tere asli jobs hain. Code 6 deta hai; users batayenge 6, 5, ya 7.
