Lesson 14 · ~6 minutes

Structure becomes navigation.

Step 1 (L13 home-map) ne faisla kiya kaunsa feature kis ghar mein — Editor ya Dashboard. Yeh Step 2 hai: woh ghar asal menu kaise bante hain — woh cheez jis par user click karta hai. Aap ne poocha "yeh sab samjhao" — toh editor ke rail ko line-by-line kholte hain.

IA ≠ navigation. IA = cheezein kaise organize hain (the home-map). Navigation = woh organization user ko kaise dikhti hai (rail, tabs, topbar). Achhi nav bас IA ko honestly mirror karti hai — kuch naya invent nahi karti. (NN/g)

Editor ki nav do layer mein hai

TopbarPoore site / session ke actions. Hamesha mojood, har mode mein same. "Main kahan hun + bahar nikalna + bhejna."
Rail (4 modes)Site ke andar aap jo kaam karte ho. Ek waqt mein ek mode. "Main abhi kya kar raha hun."

Yeh do alag sawaal hain — isliye do alag jagah. Action (publish karo) tab par; kaam (page banao) rail par. Inhe mix karna hi "messy" banata hai.

Carry this forward

Yeh poora skeleton koi naya design nahi — yeh home-map ko honestly nav mein badalna hai. Topbar = global actions (#5/#7). Rail = the 4 jobs (#10). Andar ki grouping = ek ghar (#14). Hataya gaya = not-ready (#3). Har faisla ek principle se aata hai — koi taste nahi. Yehi reason hai ke ab "kahan rakhun" ka jawab fixed hai.

Grounding: NN/g — IA vs Navigation + Tabs, Used Right. Engine: the constitution. Kuch clear nahi? Poocho — main aapka teacher hun.

Agla: yeh skeleton ko ek clickable wireframe mein badlna (asal rail + topbar), ya dashboard sidebar ko isi tarah kholna. Bolo kaunsa.
