Lesson 18 · ~5 minutes

Cover every surface — don't sketch a few.

Aap ne theek pakda: "wireframe meri existing functionality ke hisaab se nahi ban rahi — aur features ki tarah nahi, sirf functionality dikhao, poora editor redesign." Bilkul. L17 ne tareeqa sikhaya (ek screen). Yeh lesson scope theek karta hai: jab poora product redesign karna ho, wireframe ko aap ki har asli functionality ko cover karna padta hai — warna aap ek aisa product redesign kar rahe ho jo aap ke paas hai hi nahi.

Pehle: feature ≠ surface ≠ screen

Aap ki dosri baat ka dil yahi hai. Teen alag cheezein hain:

Feature

Ek capability. "Alt-text edit," "folder rename," "color token." Inventory mein 100+ hain. Yeh screen nahi.

Surface

Woh jagah jahan user kaam karta hai. "Media panel," "Inspector," "Publish flow." Ek surface 5–15 features rakhta hai.

Screen/State

Surface ki ek haalat — Media: empty / browsing / detail / uploading. Yeh aap wireframe karte ho.

Isi liye "features ki tarah nahi": agar aap 100 features ki 100 screens banaoge, woh catalog hai, product nahi. Sahi move — features ko functional surfaces mein group karo (user ke kaam se, job se), phir surface redesign karo. Editor ≈ ~15 major surfaces (coverage-map mein ≈40 finer functional rows), 100+ features nahi.

Do galtiyan jo "complete" feel karwati hain par nahi hotin

✗ Invent karna

Aisi screen banana jiska aap ke code mein koi backend/functionality nahi (forward-wireframe drift). Aap khwaab redesign karte ho, product nahi.

✓ Reverse se

Har screen kisi asli functionality se nikle (L12). Code/inventory = source of truth. Guess nahi.

✗ Sketch karna

3–4 "representative" screens bana ke "ho gaya" kehna. Baqi 10 surfaces chhoot jaate — par aap ko lagta hai poora hua.

✓ Cover karna

Har real surface list karo, har ko home do, drawn/to-draw mark karo, to-draw ko zero pe le jao.

The fix: a coverage map

Completeness ko feeling pe mat chhodo — usay countable banao. Ek table: bائیں aap ki har real functional surface (code-inventory se), daائیں uska redesign-home + kya woh wireframe mein draw ho chuki. Progress = drawn / total. Jab tak to-draw > 0, redesign adhura. Yeh aap ka definition-of-done wireframing pe lagaya gaya hai.

Maine yeh aap ke liye bana di — aap ki asli editor functionality (code-grounded: complete-feature-list + editor-orphans) → har surface ka home → drawn status. Yeh wireframe ka checklist + grounding-proof hai: → Editor functionality coverage map.

Carry this forward

Sequence wahi hai jo L12 ne di: inventory → cover → wireframe → plan-review → build. Aap ki shikayat exactly "inventory grounding" thi — isliye agla qadam screens banana nahi, pehle coverage map dekhna: kya maine aap ki saari functionality theek pakdi?
