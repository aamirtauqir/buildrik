# 0004 — Mission evolved: fix-Buildrik → become a pro product builder

**Date:** 2026-06-21
**Status:** active (mission change — confirmed pending)

## What happened
After completing the full craft arc (L1–L8 + the constitution + the verified topbar build), the learner asked the capstone question: "how do I become a good vibcoder, design like a professional PM, and improve UX/UI?" This reframes the goal from *repairing one app* to *acquiring the general discipline of building products well with AI.*

## The change
MISSION.md updated (from `0001`-era "audit + redesign Buildrik with heuristics") to: **become a pro product builder & designer — a vibcoder who directs AI like a PM and judges output like a designer.** The previously out-of-scope items (user research, measurement, product prioritisation) are now **in scope** — they're the "pro frontier" the craft alone doesn't cover.

## Why this is the right evolution
The learner has the craft (proven: they caught a real design error, built a design language, shipped a verified restructure). Craft applied to one's own guesses produces confident-but-unvalidated work. The three habits that make it pro all point away from personal taste toward the user: (1) user-first framing, (2) watching ~5 real users, (3) measuring whether a change worked. These are the deferred items now activated.

## Consequences for teaching
- New spine: the [constitution](../reference/buildrik-design-principles.html) + the loop remain the engine; the new edge is **research & measurement** (L9 + the-path reference).
- The single most important next lesson is hands-on: **run the learner's first 5-user usability test** on the core job ("publish a client's site") — turn observed stumbles into rated findings. This activates Habit 2, the highest-leverage one.
- Keep grounding in Buildrik + worked examples; the learner still wants drive-don't-quiz, Roman-Urdu, short.
- Builds on [[0003-design-is-principles-not-taste]] (named the method) → now generalising the method beyond one app.
- Primary sources added to RESOURCES: Steve Krug (Rocket Surgery / Don't Make Me Think), The Mom Test, Inspired, JTBD.
