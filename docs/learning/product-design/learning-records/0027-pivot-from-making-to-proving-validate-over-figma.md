# 0027 — The arc crosses from making to proving: founder chose validate over Figma

**Date:** 2026-06-29
**Status:** active

## What happened
After 3 codex passes left the 4 reference artifacts coherence-clean, the founder asked "next kya karna hai ab." I named the real fork instead of defaulting to their earlier stated plan (Figma-MCP recreate): **polish (Figma) vs prove (validate)**. I recommended prove and gave the honest why; the founder chose **"validate."** I built a runnable demand-gate plan (`reference/agency-validation-plan.html`) + a lesson teaching the method (`lessons/0025-validate-with-real-agencies.html`, Mom-Test-grounded).

## The teachable thing
**When the design is done and clean, the next move is not more design — it's the cheapest test that could prove it wrong.** Every prior checkpoint in this arc pointed here: L23 (only users prove correctness), LR-0017 (autoplan caught "build the wrong thing well"), LR-0008 (craft on guesses hits the wall). Three codex passes polished *coherence* — real, but coherence is internal consistency, not truth. Pouring effort into Figma would have been polishing an unvalidated artifact: making the wrong thing prettier. The founder felt the pull to "see it all in Figma," and the teacherly-correct move was to surface that Figma de-risks nothing — `design-handoff.html` already shows everything, and validation needs a real agency, not a nicer canvas.

**A good "what next?" answer names the fork the user can't see, not the path they already named.** The founder's stated plan was Figma. The honest answer was: that's a real option, but it's the lower-leverage one, and here's the higher-leverage one you didn't ask about. Driving (not quizzing) means recommending the fork, with the why, and letting them choose — they chose prove.

**The method itself is a skill the course hadn't taught.** L23 named "only users prove correctness" but stopped there. This is the missing rung: *how* to talk to customers so the data is real. The Mom Test's three rules (their life not your idea · past specifics not future opinions · compliments aren't data) turn a pitch into a diagnosis. The plan encodes the go/kill gate so the result is a decision, not a vibe: ≥3 of 5 agencies showing spent-money-or-time pain → build; fewer → the wedge isn't the wedge.
