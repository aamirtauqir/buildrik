## Call crib-card (screen pe rakho — sirf ye 5 sawal)

Rule: **past-tense pe raho. "Would you use…" KABHI nahi. Buildrik ka naam tab tak nahi jab tak wo khud na pooche.**

1. Jab ek change saare pages / saare sites pe lagana tha — **last baar** wo kaise hua? Kitna time laga, kitne tools, kaun karta hai?
2. Cross-client consistency pe **pichle mahine** kya toota? Kisi client ne pakda ya complain kiya?
3. Iske liye **abhi kya use** karte ho, aur **kitna pay** karte ho? (Webflow / Figma / manual copy-paste?)
4. **Last naya tool kab adopt kiya** — kis dard ne switch karaya?
5. (Sirf agar pain real nikle) Ye problem list mein **kahan aati hai** — top-3 mein ya "annoying but fine"?

**PULL signals (likho):** khud detail mein gaya bina pooche · "when can I get this?" · intro offer kiya · past mein paisa/time kharch kiya is pe.
**POLITE-NO signals:** "cool idea" · "sounds useful" · "definitely try karunga" — ye sab NO hain.

**Design kab dikhani hai:** call ke AAKHIR mein, sirf agar wo pooche "tum kya bana rahe ho?" — tab wedge prototype (W1→W13 click-through). Pehle kabhi nahi.
