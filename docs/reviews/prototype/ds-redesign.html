What changed · why it's calmer

1Lead with 3. Color · Type · Spacing sit at the top, always open. These are the only tokens most users edit. Everything else is one level down.
2Advanced collapses 11 → 1 line. Radius, shadow, motion, z-index, grid… all still there, but folded behind one row. Was 14 flat cards competing for attention in a 320px rail.
3Toolbar 6 → 2. Only AI + Export stay visible. Beginner/Pro, dark-mode, browse-themes move into the ⋯ menu. No more wrapping to a second row.
4Components split out. The 11 presets get their own section instead of sharing the token surface. Edit-token vs edit-component are different jobs.
5One source, visible. Header states it's synced from the workspace shared theme, with the local override called out. No guessing which "home" is real.
6Fullpage two-pane (per DESIGN.md: Design = fullpage surface). Left nav to switch, right pane to edit. Was a single cramped 320px scroll.
Current DesignSystemTab.tsx — everything in one 320px rail. 4 section-tabs, 14 token kinds, 11 presets, and a 6-control toolbar that wraps. The token editor is squeezed into the narrowest panel width in the app.
