Two things explained once: Your view (fewer or full controls) = how much you see (a preference — your view starts with fewer controls). Your role = what you can change (a permission — you edit content; structure & advanced styling are the agency's). If a control is greyed, it tells you which: "turn on full controls" or "ask an admin." (M-states · M-roles)
