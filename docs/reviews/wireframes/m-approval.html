M-approval — the optional client review gate. From the role matrix (M-roles, footnote 1): a Client-Editor can publish their own site, but the agency can flip on "client edits need approval." This is that flow — request → the agency reviews a diff → approve/reject → publish, with an audit trail. Only appears when the workspace enables it.
