Every-screen-states standard (§10) · the rule: every data screen ships 5 states, not just the happy "full" one · grounded in real load/empty/error/permission paths
The 5 mandated states
1 · Loading — skeletons, not spinners; layout doesn't jump
2 · Empty (first-run) — explain + one clear action (e.g. screen 13)
3 · Empty (filtered) — "no match" + clear-filter, distinct from first-run
4 · Error — what failed + retry + the work is safe
5 · Permission-denied — coherent read-only, not broken buttons (screen 62)
Why a standard: the happy "full" state is ~20% of real sessions. Slow networks, empty accounts, typos, viewers, outages are the other 80%. Designing them once = trust; skipping them = the app feels broken on day one.
