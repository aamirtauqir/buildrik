Topbar — designed around one idea, not assembled from parts

The idea: a topbar answers three questions, left → right — where can I go · where am I · what can I do. Two rules sharpen it: prominence = how often × how important (so the breadcrumb drops the agency — it never changes — and keeps client → site → page), and the primary button is the product's purpose — for a website builder that's Publish, not Ask AI. (v2 — corrected after the founder caught Ask AI wrongly styled as the hero.)
