# Buildrik Product Showcase Video — Design

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Generator:** Veo 3 / Flow
**Runtime:** 6 shots × 8s = 48s total
**Output format:** 1080p (and 4K master), H.264, stereo audio, 16:9
**Brief:** Industrial-grade product showcase film for Buildrik visual web builder. Cursor-as-protagonist, glass-portal signature shot, hybrid cinematic-warm + UI-cool palette, drag-drop velocity as headline feature.

## 3. World Bible

Global constants. Every shot prompt references these. Update bible → re-render shots that reference the changed token.

### 3.1 Two-world palette

| World | Used in | Hex tokens | Reads as |
|-------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Warm cinematic** | Shots 1, 2, 6 + outer half of shot 3 push-in and shot 5 pull-out | `canvas-white #f8f4f1` (paper), `ink-black #191919` (deep shadow), `paper-grey #e8e3dd` (mid-tone), `clay-orange #ee855a` (low-mix warm accent) | Quiet studio at golden hour, paper-and-pencil mood |
| **Cool UI (Buildrik real)** | Inside-glass portion of shot 3, shots 4, 5 | `#ffffff` chrome surface, `#191919` text and icons, `#2D6DFF` cobalt accent (active states), `#f5f5f7` inset panel surface | Crisp software, cobalt confidence |

**Transition logic:** glass-portal in shot 3 = palette shift. As camera passes through monitor glass, warm grade dies and cool UI grade rises across roughly 12 frames at 24fps. Mirror in shot 5 outbound (cool dies, warm returns).

### 3.2 Room geometry (warm world)

- **Set:** designer's quiet studio, shallow depth-of-field
- **Desk:** light oak wood; scattered with paper sketches, one mechanical pencil, one matte-black Wacom tablet, one half-empty dark ceramic mug
- **Monitor:** 27-inch matte ultrawide, thin black bezel, slightly tilted toward camera
- **Background:** softly out-of-focus bookshelf in lower frame, large window upper-right (light source), single trailing plant in soft focus
- **Time of day:** golden hour, low-angle warm light from camera-right; dust motes visible in beam
- **Wall:** off-white plaster
- **Negative space:** no other people, no clutter beyond intentional paper sketches, no other devices, no logos visible on hardware

### 3.3 Cursor spec (universal)

- **Shape:** macOS default arrow, 32×32 px, slightly thicker stroke than OS default for camera readability
- **Color:** body `#191919`, thin outline halo `#ffffff`
- **Trail:** 0.4s linear fade behind cursor path; tint = cobalt `#2D6DFF` at 35% opacity; gradient narrows from 8 px at head to 1 px at tail
- **Click feedback:** trail thickens to 12 px head for 80 ms on click; tiny ripple ring expands and fades over 200 ms
- **Scale by shot:**
  - Cinematic shots (1, 2, 6): cursor renders on the tiny screen-within-frame at ~3-4% of frame width
  - UI shots (4, 5): cursor renders at native UI scale
- **Position continuity:** end position of shot N = start position of shot N+1
- **Production note:** cursor + trail composited entirely in After Effects across all 6 shots. Veo prompts mention cursor *presence and position only* — they do not attempt to render the trail (Veo cursor renders are inconsistent shape).

### 3.4 Monitor spec

- **Make:** anonymous matte ultrawide, thin bezel, no logo
- **Power-on glow:** subtle bluish-white spill from screen onto desk surface; intensifies as camera approaches in shot 3 push-in
- **Reflection:** very subtle ceiling reflection on glass surface, kept low to preserve UI readability inside the screen-within-frame
- **In shots 4, 5:** monitor frame is absent — full-frame UI viewport

### 3.5 Audio motifs

| Motif | Type | Source | Used in |
|-------|------|--------|---------|
| Room tone | ambient | Veo native | Shots 1, 2, 6 |
| Pencil scratch | diegetic | Veo native | Shot 1 (subtle, distant) |
| Paper rustle, sigh | diegetic | Veo native | Shot 2 |
| Glass-pass whoosh | diegetic | Veo native | Shots 3 (entry) and 5 (exit), at the portal transition moment |
| Pad swell (musical bed) | musical | Post (After Effects audio) | Continuous shots 1-6, low mix |
| UI click | diegetic | Veo native (test); fallback Post | Shots 3, 4, 5 |
| Snap/drop | diegetic | Post sound design | Shot 4 (one per drag-drop snap) |
| Render whoosh sting | musical sting | Post | Shot 5 (page-render moment, distinct from glass-pass whoosh) |
| VO line | spoken | Veo native | Shot 6 only — "Build at the speed of taste." |
| Logo sting | musical | Post | Shot 6 closing 1.5s |

**VO direction (passed to Veo for shot 6):** warm male voice, mid-30s, dry not theatrical, neutral mid-Atlantic accent, single line, no emphasis on any word, ~0.5s pause between "speed" and "of taste".

### 3.6 Lighting tier

- **Warm world:** golden-hour soft sunlight from camera-right, key 4500K, fill negligible, dust-mote particles visible in beam, contrast medium-high
- **Cool UI world:** flat editorial UI lighting, no dramatic shadow inside chrome, cobalt accent self-illuminates from active elements
- **Glass-portal transition:** cross-fade — warm rim light dies as cool screen light rises across ~12 frames at 24fps

### 3.7 Typography

- **In-UI font (cool world):** Inter Tight (matches Buildrik real chrome)
- **Tagline + VO caption font (shot 6):** General Sans Semibold for "Build at the speed of taste."; General Sans Regular for "Buildrik."
- **Caption color in shot 6:** `#191919` on warm canvas-white background

### 3.8 Negative tokens (apply to every shot prompt)

Veo must NOT render:

- text artifacts on screens (real UI is composited in post)
- cursor with non-default shape
- visible humans (faces, hands, bodies)
- branded products on desk (Apple logos, etc. — anonymous hardware only)
- saturated or neon colors outside the palette
- high-contrast hard shadows
- camera shake or handheld feel
- film grain heavier than light 35mm grain
- speech other than the VO line in shot 6

## 5. Render workflow

Risk-first ordering, not narrative-first. Render the shots whose failure would force the largest re-budget *first*.

### 5.1 Render order

| Pass | Shot | Why this order | Stop-gate |
|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Shot 3 (glass-portal) | Highest-risk signature shot. If it fails, the entire treatment changes. | Glass-pass renders cleanly within 5 rolls? Yes → continue. No → drop to fallback ladder L2. |
| 2 | Shot 5 (reverse glass) | Mirror of shot 3. Inherits whatever solution shot 3 settles on. | Inherits shot 3's pattern. |
| 3 | Shot 1 (wide hero) | Establishes warm world. Used as image-to-video reference seed for shots 2 and 6. | Lighting and palette match bible? |
| 4 | Shot 6 (CTA + VO) | VO line is the highest-risk single moment. Render before shot 2 in case VO dictates an audio-mood adjustment that propagates. | VO line audible, paced, on-key? |
| 5 | Shot 2 (cluttered struggle) | Lowest-risk cinematic, builds on shot 1's room. | Cursor on screen reads at correct scale? |
| 6 | Shot 4 (drag-drop) | Lowest visual risk overall — Veo renders only the empty UI panel idle state. Cursor + UI screencast composited in post. | Empty UI panel renders without text artifacts? |

### 5.2 Independent renders, not scene-extend

| Approach | Used? | Why |
|----------|-------|-----|
| Independent renders | **Yes (default)** | Continuity is bible-token-driven, not scene-extend-driven |
| Scene-extend (Flow native) | No | Scene-extend forces the 8s cap on the combined clip — defeats per-shot 8s budget |
| Image-to-video seeding | Reserved fallback | Used only if continuity breaks across shots — seed shot 2 with shot 1's last frame, shot 6 with shot 5's last frame |

### 5.3 Budget estimate

- Roll count: 12-18 across all 6 shots (2-3 per shot, weighted toward shots 3, 5, 6)
- Render spend: ~$15-30 at typical Flow pricing
- Operator hours: 2-4 hours of iteration
- Post-production: 1-2 days After Effects work (Section 7 hand-off)

### 5.4 Stop-loss rules

A single shot cannot exceed:

- 6 Veo rolls without ship-criteria pass → escalate to fallback ladder
- 2 hours of operator iteration without progress → step away, re-prompt fresh
- $10 spend on a single shot → re-evaluate whether the shot can be cut or simplified

These prevent one shot from eating the whole budget.

## 6. Fallback ladder

Per-failure-mode fallbacks. Each fallback degrades gracefully — the film survives even when a layer fails.

| Failure | Severity | Trigger | Fallback |
|---------|----------|---------|----------|
| Glass-portal won't render (shot 3) | Critical | 5+ rolls of shot 3 with bent geometry, broken transition, or unconvincing pass-through | **L1:** re-prompt with shorter pass-through (3 frames not 12) and stronger bend cue. **L2:** AE morph between two Veo plates (room close-up + UI close-up). **L3:** drop to hard-cut treatment — shot 3 becomes "monitor close-up → hard cut to UI"; bible palette shift becomes a cut, not a portal. |
| Cursor drift across shots | High | Cursor shape or scale visibly different between shot 1's tiny screen-cursor and shot 6's tiny screen-cursor | All cursor rendering moves to AE; Veo prompts updated to omit cursor language entirely — only "blank screen" or "cursor area". Cursor + trail composited uniformly across all 6. |
| VO line wrong tone (shot 6) | High | Veo native VO sounds AI-flat, robotic, or wrong accent | **L1:** re-roll with stricter voice direction tokens. **L2:** strip Veo VO from shot 6, record human VO in post (~1 hour, $50-200 voice talent). **L3:** drop VO entirely, lower-third text only. |
| Pad clashes with diegetic | Medium | Pad mix muddies room tone or UI clicks | Drop pad layer entirely. Diegetic + room tone alone. Audio strategy degrades to diegetic-only. |
| Palette mismatch warm → cool | Medium | Warm shots 1, 2, 6 don't share a tint; cool shots 3-5 don't share a tint | Final color grade pass in DaVinci Resolve / AE forces LUT match. Bible re-prompts unaffected — color is post-fixable. |
| UI screencast doesn't fit monitor plate (shots 4, 5) | Low | Screen recording aspect ratio or perspective doesn't match Veo monitor plate | Re-record screencast at exact monitor aspect, or AE corner-pin the screencast to the plate. Trivial post fix. |

## 7. Post-production hand-off

Composited in After Effects after Veo plates are locked. Explicit list — nothing assumed.

1. **Cursor + light trail** across all 6 shots. Veo cursor renders are inconsistent shape; AE expression drives cursor position + trail uniformly. Cursor positions tracked against Veo plates.
2. **UI screencast on monitor in shots 4 and 5.** Actual Buildrik editor screen recording, comped onto monitor plate.
3. **Tagline + wordmark in shot 6.** General Sans typography, comped on warm canvas-white background area.
4. **Pad bed** across all 6 shots — single continuous music bed, 48s, low mix. Veo cannot reliably score continuous music.
5. **Snap/drop sfx in shot 4.** Sound design layer, 4 hits per shot.
6. **Logo sting in shot 6 closing 1.5s.**
7. **Render whoosh in shot 5** — low rumble + whoosh sting at the page-render moment.
8. **Color grade match.** Final pass to lock warm-world LUT across shots 1, 2, 6 and cool-UI LUT across shots 4, 5. Shot 3, 5 transition zones cross-graded.

## 8. Review gates and sign-off

Three gates between locks and final delivery.

| Gate | When | Reviewer | Pass criteria |
|------|------|----------|---------------|
| **Gate A — Per-shot ship** | After each shot's render Pass concludes (per Section 5.1) | Operator | Success criteria from Section 4 met. Negative tokens absent. Continuity tokens visible in render. |
| **Gate B — Six-shot continuity review** | After all 6 shots cleared Gate A | You + operator | Cursor scale consistent across cinematic shots (1, 2, 6). Palette transitions feel intentional, not jarring. Pacing crescendo readable. Pad bed (laid in temporary in AE) doesn't fight diegetic. |
| **Gate C — Final assembly sign-off** | After post-production composites land (Section 7) | You | All Section 7 hand-off items present. VO line clear. Tagline readable. 48s timeline reads as one film, not six clips. |

### 8.1 Revision triggers (objective only)

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Bible token visibly missing in shot | Re-render that shot with bible token enforced |
| Negative token visible (face, brand logo, on-screen text) | Re-render — non-negotiable |
| Pacing wrong vs crescendo curve | Adjust prompt timing language ("slow", "rapid", "deliberate"), re-render |
| Continuity break (cursor jumps, palette flip) | Patch in post if minor; re-render if structural |
| Audio layer Veo wouldn't honor | Strip Veo audio for that shot, post-mix only |

"It feels off" without one of the above triggers = stop. Either the bible is wrong (update bible, re-spec) or the trigger needs to be added (update gate criteria).
