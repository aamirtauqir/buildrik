Lesson 01 · Specification

Decision record vs design brief

Tumhara doc product ke baare mein sahi hai. Phir bhi designer usse design nahi kar sakta. Ye lesson batata hai ki dono cheezein alag kyun hain — aur ek test deta hai jisse tum apni kisi bhi line ko khud judge kar sako.

1. Ek doc 100% sahi ho sakta hai aur 0% buildable

2026-07-18 ko chaar independent audits tumhare editor docs pe chale. Chaaron ne alag-alag lens use kiya — contradictions, placement, designer-usability, cross-doc SSOT. Designer-lens wale ka verdict:

"You cannot start designing tomorrow. This is an excellent product decision record and a poor design brief."
Evidence — tumhare apne doc se
  • 912 lines mein exactly ek dimension — "4px base spacing"
  • 6 of 47 screens designable — sirf J5, kyunki sirf uske real wireframes the
  • Chrome arithmetic kabhi ki hi nahi gayi — shell apne hi viewport mein fit nahi hota tha

Gaur karo: in mein se koi bhi galat product decision nahi hai. Rail mein kya jaayega, wedge kya hai, kaunsa feature kahan rahega — sab sahi tha. Jo missing tha wo alag kism ki information hai.

Designer teen sawaal poochta hai. Tum sirf pehle ka jawab de rahe the.

Isiliye doc "complete" mehsoos hota tha. Ek poora sawaal shaandaar tareeke se answer ho raha tha — aur baaki do ka wajood hi nahi tha.

2. The test — ek hi sawaal jo sab decide karta hai

The two-designer test

"Kya do qaabil designers ye line padhkar do alag cheezein bana sakte hain?"

Agar haan → ye spec nahi hai, ye decision hai. Agar nahi → ye spec hai.

Ye test isliye kaam karta hai kyunki wo tumhari niyat ko ignore karta hai. Tumhe pata hai tumhara matlab kya tha. Designer ko nahi. Test sirf ye poochta hai ki likhe hue lafz kitne rasta chhodte hain.

Audit ne tumhare doc mein 9 aisi lines nikaali (A1–A9). Ek misaal:

"Sits with the canvas"          ← canvas toolbar ke baare mein

Designer A padhta hai: canvas ke neeche full-width bar
Designer B padhta hai: canvas ke upar floating pill (Figma jaisa)

Do alag product. Ek hi line se.

3. Ab tum karo — 6 asli lines

Har line tumhare apne docs se hai. Faisla karo: BUILDABLE ya AMBIGUOUS.

4. Ambiguous ko buildable kaise banate ho

Har baar wahi paanch cheezein missing hoti hain. Ye tumhari checklist hai:

#Add karoTest
1Number — width, height, row density, gapKya main ise graph paper pe naap sakta hoon?
2States — empty · loading · error · full · gatedHar state ke liye ek line copy likhi hai?
3Behaviour — click pe kya, dobara click pe kyaToggle hai ya nahi? Likha hai?
4Real content — asli naam, asli lambai40-page site mein kya hota hai?
5Position — kis cheez ke relative, kitna door"Ke saath" nahi — "bottom-centred, 24 from bottom"
Sabse bada leverage — frame pehle

Doc ke 41 undesignable screens mein se zyadatar ek hi cheez ke bachche hain: drawer. Insert · Layers · Pages · Media · Content · Brand — sab wahi 320px frame hai, andar alag saamaan.

Isliye ek shell wireframe (asli viewport pe, har dimension labelled) ne akele 6 panels ko designable bana diya. Pehle frame settle karo, phir cargo. Ye Brad Frost ka atomic-design principle hai — chhoti cheez pehle, badi usse bani.

5. Ek warning — numbers jhoot bolte hain

Maine shell wireframe likha jisme har dimension thi. Audit ne usme bhi galti nikaali:

topbar 56 + banner 40 + tabs 36 + band 768 + footer 32 = 932
viewport = 900

...footer hi drop kar diya tha.

Sabak: dimension likhna kaafi nahi — jodna bhi padta hai. Har layout number ka ek arithmetic check hota hai. Vertical: sab bands ka jod = viewport height. Horizontal: chrome + canvas = viewport width. Agar jod nahi milta, spec jhooth hai — chahe har number "reasonable" lage.

Aage kya

  • Reference: Spec completeness checklist — har screen ship karne se pehle isse guzaaro.
  • Primary source: Brad Frost, Atomic Design — free online at atomicdesign.bradfrost.com. Chapter 2 padho (the atomic methodology). Wahi "frame pehle, cargo baad mein" wala idea hai jo tumhare 6 panels ko ek saath unlock karta hai.
  • Agla lesson: states — har screen ke liye empty/loading/error likhna. Ye wo cheez hai jo tumhare docs mein sirf naam tha, spec nahi.

Kuch bhi clear na ho to poochho. Main tumhara teacher hoon — koi bhi line utha kar poochho "ye buildable hai ya nahi", main uspe chalunga.
